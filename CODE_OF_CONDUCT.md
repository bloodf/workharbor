# Code of conduct

WorkHarbor welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels, languages, and perspectives. Everyone participating in project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces is expected to help keep them respectful and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Be constructive, specific, and patient when giving or receiving feedback.
- Respect different communication styles and levels of technical experience.
- Respect privacy. Share only information you have permission to disclose.
- Keep discussion relevant to the project and accept reasonable moderation decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, repeated disruption, and publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable. Neither are personal attacks or retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Raising a concern

The repository maintainer, [@bloodf](https://github.com/bloodf), is responsible for project moderation.

- If a concern can be described safely in public, open a minimal [issue](https://github.com/bloodf/workharbor/issues/new/choose) or flag the relevant discussion to the maintainer. Include only the context necessary to locate the problem.
- If a report requires private evidence, use the “Private contact request” issue form to ask for a private conduct-reporting channel first. Do not include names, allegations, personal details, or evidence in that public request. Wait for a verified channel before sharing them. No dedicated private conduct-reporting address is listed here.
- You may also use GitHub's own reporting tools for abuse or a violation of GitHub's policies, including when your concern involves the maintainer. You do not need to confront the person involved.

Do not use a vulnerability reporting form for an unrelated conduct dispute. Security reports belong under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may request a change in behavior, edit or remove inappropriate content, close or lock a discussion, or restrict participation where necessary. The response should reflect the severity, repetition, and effect of the behavior, rather than disagreement about a technical opinion.

Reports should be handled discreetly, with information shared only as needed to understand and address the concern. Public issue comments are not confidential. Do not post sensitive evidence there, and do not assume that a private reporting channel or a response deadline has been guaranteed.
